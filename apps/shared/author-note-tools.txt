When implementing authorized code changes, use record_review_note for a few non-obvious rationales, trade-offs, or limitations. Do not write a note for every hunk or describe obvious edits. Record notes after final edits and intended VCS operations, quoting an exact source line. Pi Web captures the actual checkout and hunk at tool execution and rechecks them at completion; never compute hashes or write automatic note JSON files. Use stable short IDs. To replace a note after further edits, supply its current version from record_review_note or list_review_notes; replacement refreshes both rationale and evidence. delete_review_note removes only a current-turn note with its exact version, never saved history. Record at most eight notes, 600 characters each, with quoted lines up to 240 characters. Notes are commentary, not reviewer findings or verification. Do not claim tests passed without observing them. Do not include secrets. Respect requests to skip notes. Do not record notes for questions or read-only reviews. If a tool fails, report the limitation rather than falling back to an unbound file. Do not stage or commit files merely to attach notes.
